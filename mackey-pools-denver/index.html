Section 6: Prioritized Action Plan
Ranked by impact on local visibility and lead generation
Critical — Do This Week
1. Add Phone Number to Google Business Profile
Log into GBP Manager (business.google.com) and add (303) 829-2569 as the primary phone. A GBP without a phone number looks abandoned to Google and severely limits local pack eligibility. This is a 5-minute fix with potentially outsized ranking impact.
Time: 5 minutes · Platform: Google Business Profile Manager · Impact: Very High
2. Respond to the 1-Star Review Professionally
Write a calm, professional owner response acknowledging the reviewer's experience. Silence is far worse than a diplomatic reply. Suggested response: "We take the quality of every project seriously and regret that you had this experience. We would welcome the opportunity to discuss this further — please contact Ken directly at (303) 829-2569." Even if the dispute was complex, responding shows prospective clients that you are engaged and professional.
Time: 15 minutes · Platform: GBP Manager · Impact: High — First thing prospects see
3. Fix the LocalBusiness Schema Phone and ZIP Code
In Wix, go to SEO Settings and find the LocalBusiness structured data block. Change telephone from "123 456 7890" to "(303) 829-2569" and postalCode from "80014" (Aurora) to the correct ZIP for your service area. This schema is being read by Google to verify business contact data — fake data actively hurts entity trust.
Time: 15 minutes · Platform: Wix SEO Advanced Settings · Impact: High
4. Rewrite the Services Page Immediately
The /services page currently contains Wix default text and is indexed by Google. Replace with at least 500 words describing each service: Custom Pool Design and Construction, Custom Spas and Hot Tubs, Water Features, Pool Covers, and Pool Renovations. Include the Denver/Colorado service area context. Add real photos. This single page fix removes active harmful content from Google's index.
Time: 2-3 hours · Platform: Wix Editor · Impact: Critical
5. Launch an Immediate Review Generation Campaign
Contact every past client from the last 2-3 years by phone or email. Send a short, personal message: "Hi [Name], I hope you're still enjoying your pool! If you have a moment, I'd really appreciate a Google review — it means a lot to a small family business. Here's the link: [GBP review link]." Target 10 reviews in the first 30 days. Rocky Mountain Pool Builders ranks #2 in the local pack with just 1 review at 5 stars — you only need the rating to improve to get back in the pack.
Time: Ongoing · Impact: Critical — Nothing else matters more than this right now
High Priority — Within 30 Days
6. Add H1 Tag to Homepage
In Wix, designate the primary headline as H1 (not H2 or H5). Recommended H1: "Custom Pool & Spa Builder Serving Denver, CO." Currently the phone number occupies the H2 position, which wastes the heading hierarchy. Move the phone to the header/navigation area instead.
Time: 15 minutes · Impact: High
7. Expand Homepage Content to 800+ Words
168 words is far too thin for a competitive keyword. Add: an intro paragraph with primary keywords (custom pool builder Denver CO), a services overview section, a "Why Choose Mackey" section highlighting 20-year owner-operated differentiator, a service areas list, and a clear CTA with phone number. Competitors average 800-1,500 words on their homepages.
Time: 3-4 hours · Impact: High
8. Complete the GBP Profile Fully
Write a 750-character GBP business description. Add services: Custom Pool Construction, Custom Spa Construction, Water Features, Pool Renovations, Pool Covers. Add secondary categories: Hot Tub Store, Swimming Pool Repair Service. Configure service area to include all target cities. Upload 20+ high-quality project photos with descriptive filenames.
Time: 2-3 hours · Impact: High
9. Resolve NAP Inconsistency Across All Platforms
Decide on one canonical phone number and address. Update the Yellow Pages listing (currently showing (303) 923-3950 and Englewood address). Claim and verify the Yelp listing. Update the schema ZIP code and phone. Ensure every platform shows the exact same business name format, phone, and address.
Time: 1-2 hours · Impact: High
10. Add Image Alt Text Throughout Site
All portfolio photos currently have no alt text. In Wix, add descriptive alt text to each image: e.g., "Custom negative edge pool Cherry Hills Village Denver by Mackey Pools & Spas." This is a missed keyword signal for Google Images and improves accessibility score (currently 86/100).
Time: 1-2 hours · Impact: Medium-High
11. Start Weekly Google Posts
Post at least once per week in GBP: share project completion photos, seasonal pool tips (winterizing a Colorado pool, opening your pool in spring), or a "pool of the month" feature. This signals to Google that the business is active — a local ranking factor. Competitors who post consistently maintain better visibility than dormant listings.
Time: 20 min/week · Impact: Medium
Medium Priority — 30-60 Days
12. Create Service Area / Location Pages
Aquality Pools ranks across the Denver metro with 6 dedicated location pages (Denver, Boulder, Broomfield, Erie, Longmont, Cherry Hills Village). Create 5-8 pages targeting: Cherry Hills Village Pool Builder, Englewood CO Pool Builder, Lone Tree Pool Builder, Castle Rock Pool Builder, Greenwood Village Pool Builder. Each page needs unique 300+ word content, embedded Google Map, local testimonial, and LocalBusiness schema.

13. Get Listed on BBB, Houzz, and Angi
The business is not found on BBB (404 error), Houzz (404 error), or confirmed on Angi. Integrity Pool Builders prominently displays its BBB A+ rating as a trust signal. Claim listings on: BBB (bbb.org/western-mountain-pacific/accredited-business), Houzz, Angi, Thumbtack, and Nextdoor. Use consistent NAP on all.

14. Remove or Complete Orphaned "Copy-Of" Pages
Three pages in the sitemap have "copy-of" in the URL (/copy-of-spas, /copy-of-build-process, /copy-of-pools). Delete these Wix draft pages or 301 redirect them to their canonical counterparts. They fragment crawl budget and potentially send duplicate content signals.

15. Publish Pool Cost Guide for Denver
The related searches "pool builder denver co cost" and "pool builder denver co reviews" appear consistently. Create a page: "How Much Does a Custom Pool Cost in Denver, CO?" addressing price ranges for concrete, fiberglass, and vinyl pools. SERP data shows a concrete custom pool ranges $65k-$120k. Build it into the project close-out process: at the final walkthrough, give Ken a card or send a text with the Google review link. Follow up 1 week later. Respond to every review within 48 hours — positive and negative. Goal: 2-3 new reviews per month once the pipeline system is running.
