8. Root Cause Analysis & Recommendations

Probable Causes (ranked by impact):

  1. "supply velocity" branded search decline (HIGHEST IMPACT): This single query drove 10 of the 13 lost homepage clicks. Impressions dropped 30% (fewer people searching the brand) and CTR fell from 61%→44% despite holding position #1. The SERP for this query now includes PAA results about "supply chain velocity" as a concept, creating brand/concept confusion. This is primarily a brand awareness issue, not an SEO issue.
  2. AI Overviews absorbing clicks (HIGH IMPACT): Google's AI Overviews now appear for "lean supply chain consulting" and "lean six sigma consulting" — two queries highly relevant to the homepage. These AI Overviews are massive (full-page, with firm recommendations and pricing) and SV is not cited in any of them. This directly reduces organic CTR even if position is maintained.
  3. March 2026 core update impact (HIGH IMPACT): The core update (Mar 27 - Apr 8) reshuffled consulting-niche rankings. incito.com now outranks SV for "lean supply chain consulting". The position worsening from ~22 to ~30+ is consistent with core update re-evaluations of E-E-A-T signals.
  4. Homepage content changes (MEDIUM IMPACT): The restructuring between February and May changed keyword signals. Renaming services and adding industry sections diluted the page's focus on "lean supply chain consulting" and "lean six sigma consulting". The page became longer and broader rather than more focused on high-value queries.
  5. May 2026 core update (DEVELOPING): Started May 21 — too recent to fully assess, but may compound March update effects.
